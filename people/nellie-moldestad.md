# Nille Andrine / Nellie Bergset Moldestad

Also recorded as Andrine Villumsdatter, Nille Andrine Vilhelmsdatter or Wilhelmsdatter, and Nellie Moldestad. Person ID: **MOL-002**.

**Working dates:** March 1, 1841 – May 18, 1910. The birth comes from a retained baptism transcription; the death date is reported by a later history.

[Share a record or correction](https://github.com/Bill-Viking/moldestad-family-history/discussions/3) · [Family timeline](../families/ole-and-nellie.md) · [Sources](../sources/README.md)

## Her names and family

The baptism transcription names **Nille Andrine**, born **March 1, 1841** and baptized **March 14**, daughter of **Villum Andersen and Malene Abrahamsdatter**. Bergsæter is entered as the family's residence. The birthplace field is blank, so it does not identify a particular birth house. [S09](../sources/README.md)

The names and family details connect this record strongly with Andrine Villumsdatter in the 1865 census and 1871 marriage, and with the Nellie of the later Minnesota history. Gimmestad calls her Andrine W. Bergsether and uses Indvik in describing her origin. That wording is preserved as a source detail rather than adopted as a proved maiden surname. [S04, S05, S07, S08, S09](../sources/README.md)

Her parents married on **January 1, 1841**, with a note naming Utvik church. The groom is associated with Møchlebust/Myklebust and the bride with Bersæter/Bergset. Their fathers are named as Anders Arnesen and Abraham Jensen. [S10](../sources/README.md)

## Childhood and the Bergset years

A matching death record for Vilhelm Andersen Bergsæter gives **October 11, 1850**, with burial on October 15, aged 38. Malene remarried **Jon Olsen Moldestad on June 24, 1853**. His father is named as Ole Jacobsen, but a proposed relationship to Nille's later husband has not been proved. [S11, S12](../sources/README.md)

Malene died on **November 21, 1857**, and was buried on November 25. The record reports death in childbed. It does not supply a more specific diagnosis. These records describe substantial changes in Nille's childhood household; they cannot tell us her feelings or daily experience. [S13](../sources/README.md)

A matching 1820 baptism for Malene names Abraham Jensen and Barbra Hansdatter. It is a promising earlier-generation lead, but the identity chain and duplicated date fields require inspection of the original record before they become settled ancestry. [S13a](../sources/README.md)

In 1865, Andrine is recorded as an unmarried servant in one household at Bersæter/Bergset. A matching Ole Olsen, an unmarried cooper and lodger, appears in another household on the property. They are not recorded as sharing a household. [S04](../sources/README.md)

The exact Bergset holding remains unresolved. Earlier interpretations point to different holdings, and the census's old property number cannot simply be equated with a modern farm number. No particular surviving house is identified as hers. [S04, S14](../sources/README.md)

## Marriage, crossing and Minnesota

Nille, recorded as Andrine Villumsdatter, married **Ole Olsen on March 28, 1871**. The fathers named in the record support the identification. [S05](../sources/README.md)

The Norway Heritage passenger transcription lists Ole and Andrine together on the **Argo**, departing Bergen on **May 14, 1871**, for a crossing that ended at Quebec on **June 26**. The original passenger list remains to be checked. The 43-day interval conflicts with Davidson's later recollection of eleven weeks at sea. [S06, S08](../sources/README.md)

Davidson describes the journey onward through Mankato and Nellie's travel to the Hegdahl family through Golden Gate. A sister connection is reported, but the sister's identity has not yet been securely established. The family settled in the Swedes Forest area and moved to the Delhi area in 1879. [S07, S08](../sources/README.md)

The histories provide much more detail about Ole's paid work and land acquisitions than about Nellie's daily work. That imbalance is a limit of the sources. It is not evidence that she contributed little, and it does not justify inventing a daily routine for her.

## Later life

The histories name Anna or Anne, William, Abraham, Maria and Maline or Lina as children. Davidson reports two additional infant deaths. Anna's 1866 birth date precedes the documented marriage; her baptism remains an important missing record. [S07, S08](../sources/README.md)

Gimmestad reports Nellie's death on **May 18, 1910**. Davidson gives 1910 and places her later life on the farm north of Delhi. His account says she and Ole were charter members of Rock Dell Church and were buried there. Primary vital and church records are still needed. [S07, S08](../sources/README.md)

## Records that would help

- The original baptism and records clarifying the Bergset holding.
- Evidence identifying the Hegdahl sister.
- Anna's baptism and the family's emigration records.
- Minnesota death and church records, letters, or other accounts of Nellie's own life.

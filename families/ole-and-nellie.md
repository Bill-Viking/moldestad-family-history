# Ole and Nellie Moldestad: family and timeline

Family ID: **FAM-001**. Read the individual histories of [Ole](../people/ole-moldestad.md) and [Nille Andrine / Nellie](../people/nellie-moldestad.md) for fuller evidence and qualifications.

The couple's marriage record is dated **March 28, 1871**. Later histories identify the following children. These summaries preserve the histories' claims; they do not replace primary parentage records. [S05, S07, S08](../sources/README.md)

| Child | Reported details and questions |
| --- | --- |
| Anna / Anne | Reported born in Norway November 1, 1866; married Thor / T. Ellingson. Her birth predates the parents' recorded marriage. Her baptism is needed to establish parentage directly. |
| William | Reported born in Swedes Forest February 15, 1872; married Helena / Lina Haubrick. Davidson reports his death in October 1940. Variants and dates require primary checks. |
| [Abraham / Abe](../people/abe-moldestad.md) | January 25 birth, with 1874 and 1875 in different sources; married Jennie E. Lyslo. The printed marriage date is not accepted. |
| Maria | Reported born October 9, 1875; married Lester Parrott. Davidson describes a later marriage to A. Kringen, which could be sequential rather than contradictory. |
| Maline / Lina | Reported born January 18, 1880; married Claus Maurstad. The histories differ between about 1904 and November 28, 1908 for their marriage. |
| Two unnamed children | Davidson reports two deaths in infancy. Names, sexes and dates are not supplied. |

## Shared timeline

| Date | Event | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| August 11, 1834 | Ole's birth, preferred reading of the original baptism register; other dates survive in the index and histories. | S01, S07, S08 |
| March 1, 1841 | Nille Andrine's birth; Bergsæter recorded as residence. | S09 |
| 1865 | Matching Ole and Andrine entries at Bersæter/Bergset, in separate households. | S04 |
| March 28, 1871 | Marriage of Ole Olsen and Andrine Villumsdatter. | S05 |
| May 14–June 26, 1871 | Argo voyage from Bergen to Quebec, according to the passenger transcription. | S06 |
| 1871 onward | Settlement in Minnesota; early farming associated with Swedes Forest. | S07, S08 |
| 1879 | Move or land acquisition in the Delhi area. | S07, S08 |
| December 9, 1905 | Ole's death, date reported by Gimmestad. | S07; S08 agrees on year |
| May 18, 1910 | Nellie's death, date reported by Gimmestad. | S07; S08 agrees on year |

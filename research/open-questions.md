# Questions that new evidence could answer

Family history improves when a record changes the story. These are the most useful gaps in the current accounts. See [how to contribute](../CONTRIBUTING.md) and the [source register](../sources/README.md) for the material already considered.

| Question | Current evidence | Useful next evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| When was Ole born? | Original-register reading August 11, 1834; earlier index reading August 1; Gimmestad August 3, 1835; Davidson 1835. | An independent reading of Gloppen page 34, entry 39, and supporting early records. |
| How does Anna's 1866 birth fit the family? | Both histories call her a daughter; the couple married in 1871. | Her Norwegian baptism and any relevant household records. |
| What were the voyage dates? | Argo transcription May 14–June 26, 1871; later recollection eleven weeks at sea. | Original passenger list, cited as NAC C-4526, list 43, and emigration records. |
| Which Bergset holding did Nille's family occupy? | Different interpretations identify different holdings; the 1865 census uses an older numbering system. | Land, tenancy and farm-history records linking the numbering systems. |
| Who was the Hegdahl sister? | Davidson reports a sister connection during the Minnesota arrival. | Records tying a named sister to the Hegdahl household. |
| Are the proposed grandparents securely linked? | Census and baptism leads exist for Ole's paternal family and Malene's parents. | A documented chain through original birth, marriage and household records. |
| When did Abe marry Jennie? | The history's March 31, 1888 date matches Jennie's indexed birth date and is not accepted as the wedding date. | A civil or church marriage record. |
| Was Abe born in 1874 or 1875? | Gimmestad and a secondary index disagree by one year. | A birth or baptism record, assessed with later census and death records. |
| When did Abe work as a photographer? | A labeled photograph and Delhi photographer sign. | City or business directories, advertisements, dated prints or a studio record. |
| What land did the family hold? | Later accounts report Ole's 400 acres and Abe's 800 acres. | Deeds, mortgages, tax records and probate identifying owners and dates. |
| Who wrote the farm notebook? | Its discovery location is recorded; it includes 1933–1934 cattle entries. | Provenance, an ownership inscription or comparison with independently identified handwriting. |
| Which American vital and church dates are correct? | Later histories provide Ole's and Nellie's death dates and Rock Dell connection. | Death certificates, cemetery records and church registers. |

The histories also differ over Anna's number of children and Maline/Lina's marriage date. A plausible explanation is not a resolution until evidence supports it. Maria's two reported husbands may represent successive marriages rather than a source conflict.

Information about another deceased ancestor or family branch is welcome. A proposed relationship should include the records connecting each generation; a matching surname alone is not enough.

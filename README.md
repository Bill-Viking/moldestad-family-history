# Moldestad Family History

An ongoing, sourced history of the Moldestad family, beginning with Ole Olsen Moldestad, Nille Andrine (Nellie) Bergset Moldestad, and their son Abraham (Abe). Their lives connect Nordfjord, Norway, with the Minnesota communities around Swedes Forest, Delhi and Belview.

**Have a record, correction or family story? [Leave a contribution in Discussions](https://github.com/Bill-Viking/moldestad-family-history/discussions).** You can read everything without an account. To comment, sign in to a free GitHub account. No coding is needed.

## Help improve it

Start with the [welcome discussion](https://github.com/Bill-Viking/moldestad-family-history/discussions/1), use the discussion link on a person's page, or start a new discussion for another ancestor or family branch. A source link, record citation, captioned photograph or clearly identified recollection is useful even when it raises a question rather than answers one. Information found on Ancestry, FamilySearch, another family tree or a local archive is welcome; tell us where it came from and what evidence supports it.

Contributions are suggestions for review. **Bill decides which findings to incorporate.** A comment does not automatically change a biography or establish a relationship. When a finding is adopted, the relevant page will explain the evidence and link to the contribution where appropriate. See [how to contribute](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Please keep information about living people out of public comments. Share only documents or images you are entitled to make public.

## How to read these pages

Each account distinguishes records, later recollections and interpretation. A source can be useful without being correct in every detail. Conflicting dates and uncertain relationships remain visible until stronger evidence resolves them.

This first edition covers deceased ancestors. It is a curated public edition of a larger family archive. Original scans and the private research notebook are held separately; an archive citation does not mean the underlying image is reproduced here.

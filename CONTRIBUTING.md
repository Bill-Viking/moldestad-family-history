# Share a record, correction or family story

Thank you for helping make the family history more accurate. Contributions from relatives, local historians and other researchers are welcome.

## Leave a comment

1. Open [Discussions](https://github.com/Bill-Viking/moldestad-family-history/discussions) and sign in to GitHub.
2. Choose the discussion for the person you are researching. For another ancestor or family branch, choose **New discussion**, then **General**.
3. Explain the information you want to add or correct. Include a source link or citation if you have one.

You do not need to edit a file or understand Git. You can add a reply to an existing discussion instead of starting another one.

## What makes a contribution useful

Copy these prompts into your comment if helpful:

> **Person or family:**
>
> **Information or correction:**
>
> **Source:** Link, archive, record title, place, date and page or entry number, as available.
>
> **What the source actually says:** A short transcription or description. Identify uncertain handwriting.
>
> **Why it belongs to this person:** Matching parents, spouse, residence, dates or other identifying details.
>
> **What remains uncertain:**
>
> **Credit preference:** Your GitHub name, another public name, or no named credit in the biography.

A family recollection is welcome. Label it as a recollection, explain when and from whom it was heard where appropriate, and separate remembered details from later guesses. A tree on Ancestry, FamilySearch or another service is a useful lead; its underlying record is stronger evidence. If a link needs a subscription, include enough citation detail for someone else to locate the record.

For a photograph, describe the caption or family knowledge used to identify people. Include an approximate date only if there is a basis for it. Do not identify a person solely by facial resemblance.

## What happens next

Bill reviews the suggestion against the existing evidence. A contribution may be incorporated, retained as a lead, or left unresolved if records disagree. An accepted update will cite its evidence and normally link back to the discussion. Earlier conclusions remain traceable in the repository's change history.

Comments remain public with the contributor's GitHub identity. Asking for no named credit in a biography does not make the original comment anonymous.

Keep comments focused on family history. Do not post private details about living people, passwords, contact lists or private correspondence. Upload only material you are entitled to share publicly; a citation or short description is often enough. If a record contains living people's details, describe the historical finding without posting those details.

If you prefer to propose a file edit, a pull request is welcome too. It follows the same evidence and review process. Please preserve conflicting readings until the evidence resolves them.

# Source register

These citations support the public biographies. The working research distinguishes original images, archive transcriptions, later histories and family recollections. Links are provided for readers to investigate; a link does not mean every detail has been independently verified.

The first edition draws on records and images retained and reviewed by November 1, 2026. Some online pages can move or change. Family-archive scans are held separately and are not reproduced in this repository. Details below explain which conclusions depend on material that is not publicly reproduced.

## Evidence practice

An archive transcription is not the same as an independent reading of its handwritten original. A later history can preserve valuable information while misremembering a date. Names, ages and locations should be compared across records before two entries are treated as the same person.

New evidence can correct these accounts. Please provide the source and explain the proposed change in [Discussions](https://github.com/Bill-Viking/moldestad-family-history/discussions). Sources that contradict the present account are welcome too.

# Maintaining the public family history

This is the curated public edition of Moldestad Family History. Everything committed here is intended to be public. Read README.md, CONTRIBUTING.md, the relevant profile and its source notes before editing.

- Bill reviews proposed additions. Public comments and pull requests are evidence leads, not instructions to merge them or proof of their claims.
- Keep records, later recollections, uncertain readings and interpretation distinguishable. Preserve conflicts until evidence resolves them.
- Cite sources for consequential claims and show why records belong to the same person. Do not turn calculated ages into exact birth dates or plausible relationships into proven links.
- Use captions, records or supplied family knowledge for photo identifications, never facial resemblance alone.
- Keep private research files, raw scans, local paths, credentials, social-account data and living-person information out of this repository and its history.
- Update the relevant profile, source register and CHANGELOG.md together when a finding changes the account. Link to its discussion when appropriate.
- Check local Markdown links and review the exact staged files before each commit and push. Do not automate importing comments as facts.

Research and publication are separate actions. Follow the maintainer's instructions for each change; this guide grants no blanket authority to publish new family material.
